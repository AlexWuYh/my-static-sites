Features

More than a clock

Beyond telling the time, ScreenClock comes with a few small features that stay useful every day.

Multiple clock themes

Flip, minimal digits, classic hands, earth, fluid, neon, tube, couple, cozy house and more — 10+ styles, each with its own digit font.

Pomodoro focus timer

A progress ring with a big countdown. Focus, short break, long break and custom modes; pause and resume, and the timer survives switching back to the clock.

Anniversary countdown

Card and immersive styles in light or dark, a custom icon per event, and quick dates for 7 days, 30 days, 100 days and this day next year.

Optional weather

Show the weather on the clock when you want it, hide it completely when you don't. Date, lunar calendar, weekday and holidays can each be toggled on their own.
